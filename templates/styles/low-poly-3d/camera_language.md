# Low-Poly 3D Camera Language

- Use stable, game-cinematic framing.
- Prioritize wide establishing shots, medium character shots, and controlled push-ins.
- Keep camera movement smooth and motivated: slow dolly, gentle orbit, lateral track.
- Avoid jitter, whip pans, shaky handheld movement, and excessive lens distortion.
- Preserve readable silhouettes and blocky form language.
- Favor compositions that emphasize angular surfaces, planar shadows, and simple depth cues.
