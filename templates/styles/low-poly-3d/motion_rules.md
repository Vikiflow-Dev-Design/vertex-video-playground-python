# Low-Poly 3D Motion Rules

- Motion should feel like a polished game cinematic, not documentary footage.
- Animate with deliberate, restrained movement.
- Keep character silhouettes readable at all times.
- Use small gestures, slow head turns, and simple environmental motion.
- Avoid exaggerated cloth simulation, skin realism, or noisy micro-movement.
- Favor clean transitions and stable scene geography.
