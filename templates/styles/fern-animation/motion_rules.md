# Motion Rules — Fern-Inspired Documentary Animation

- Use restrained movement with clear physical motivation.
- Keep recurring characters' silhouette, wardrobe, face shape, and gesture vocabulary stable.
- Use layered parallax and small environmental motion to make still compositions feel alive.
- Limit each short clip to one primary action and one camera movement.
- Carry motion across split clips; do not reset the character or environment between parts.
- Let scene transitions use match cuts, object movement, sound bridges, or environmental consequences.
- Avoid rubbery limbs, floating props, sudden wardrobe changes, uncontrolled crowds, and random camera shake.
