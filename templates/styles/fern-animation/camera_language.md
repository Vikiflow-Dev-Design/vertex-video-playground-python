# Camera Language — Fern-Inspired Documentary Animation

Use a motivated shot progression rather than random variety.

- Establishing: wide, calm, orient the viewer in time and place.
- Observation: medium framing, hold on a specific human action.
- Evidence: controlled close-up of an object, document, trace, or environment.
- Revelation: slow push-in or measured track toward the visual hook.
- Consequence: pull back, change the environment, or match-cut to the result.

Preserve screen direction, eyelines, horizon level, and light direction across adjacent shots. Short clips should use simple movement; longer clips may contain one deliberate action plus one camera movement. Avoid gratuitous zooms, handheld shake, whip pans, and unmotivated aerials.
