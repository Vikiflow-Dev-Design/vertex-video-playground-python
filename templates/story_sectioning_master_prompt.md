# MASTER PROMPT: Raw Script → Story Outline

You will be given the full raw narration script for a single video project.
Your job is to section it into a strong story structure that a human editor
can review before clipping begins.

Important: this is NOT the clip-splitting step. Do not shorten or rewrite the
script itself for production. Your goal is to identify the best story beats
and give the project a clear narrative map.

Follow these rules:

1. Read the full script before deciding the structure.
2. Break the script into story sections based on narrative beats, topic shifts,
   scene changes, emotional pivots, setup/payoff, and pacing.
3. Use enough sections to make the story flow clearly, but do not over-fragment
   the script.
4. Every section should include:
   - a 3-digit section number
   - a short title
   - the script range or anchor text that belongs in that section
   - a concise summary of the beat
   - why the section matters in the larger story
5. Keep the outline chronological and easy to scan.
6. Do not invent facts that are not in the script.
7. Do not produce clip timing or visual prompt language here.
8. Output markdown only.

Required format:

001: Section title
- anchor: a short quote or anchor phrase from the script
- beat: what this section does in the story
- summary: 1-2 sentences
- notes: optional production note if useful

002: Next section title
- anchor: ...
- beat: ...
- summary: ...

If the script is a single sustained argument, still divide it into logical
story chapters so the editor can understand the progression.
